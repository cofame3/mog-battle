Our Technology

Omogle uses TensorFlow.js and the Face Mesh model to perform real-time facial analysis entirely within the user's browser. This approach eliminates the need for server-side image processing, ensuring complete privacy and zero-latency results.

  • Precision: We use a 468-point mesh to analyze every micro-fold of your face. The algorithm measures inter-pupillary distance, philtrum length, jawline angles, and nose-to-chin proportions with sub-pixel accuracy.
  • Speed: Analysis happens in real-time directly in your browser using WebGL. The TensorFlow.js model loads once and runs at 30+ frames per second, providing instant feedback during battles.
  • Security: Your data is encrypted and processed locally. We never upload facial images to our servers. The only data transmitted is the numerical analysis result — never your actual photos.

How Facial Analysis Scoring Works

Our scoring system evaluates six distinct facial parameters, each contributing to the overall aesthetic rating:

  • Symmetry (20%): Measures the bilateral symmetry of facial features by comparing left and right halves. Perfect symmetry is rare, but higher symmetry scores correlate with greater perceived attractiveness.
  • Jawline (20%): Evaluates the definition, angle, and width of the jawline. A well-defined jaw with angles close to 120-130 degrees typically scores highest.
  • Eyes (20%): Analyzes canthal tilt (the angle from inner to outer corner), eye width-to-height ratio, and orbital spacing relative to face width.
  • Nose (15%): Measures nasal bridge width, tip projection, and the ratio of nose width to face width. The ideal ratio is approximately 1:5.
  • Skin (15%): Assesses skin texture uniformity and clarity based on pixel analysis of the facial surface.
  • Proportions (10%): Evaluates overall facial thirds (forehead, mid-face, lower face) and their relation to the golden ratio.
